Whistle host pipeline
---------------------
notes/whistle_tract.py adapts the host frontend, engram lookup and DTW word
timestamp algorithms from mrfakename/whistle-ONNX, revision
14fc92cb09ad5c271fe9a4ec4be70274790050d2, licensed under Apache-2.0.
Source: https://huggingface.co/mrfakename/whistle-ONNX
License: https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0
The ASR model is Cactus Compute Whistle, not a Whisper transcription model.

Smart Turn
----------
The downloaded Smart Turn v3 ONNX model is distributed by onnx-community,
based on pipecat-ai/smart-turn-v3, under the BSD-2-Clause license.
Source: https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/smart-turn-v3-ONNX
Inference/preprocessing reference: https://github.com/pipecat-ai/smart-turn
Its Whisper-derived encoder is used solely for end-of-turn classification.
